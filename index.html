Ganciclovir and Valganciclovir for CMV Infection in Immunosuppressed Patients

Dr. Özgür Karcıoğlu & Dr. Satuk Buğra Han Bozatlı  — select any box below to read the reasoning and sources behind that step.

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24324068

Educational / academic reference tool, not a substitute for institutional protocol or clinical judgement at the bedside. This algorithm applies to immunosuppressed patients (solid organ transplant and haematopoietic cell transplant recipients, people with advanced HIV, haematological malignancy, or other causes of impaired cell-mediated immunity) at risk of, or with, cytomegalovirus infection or disease; it should not be extrapolated to immunocompetent patients. This tool's reference list is reproduced directly from the source manuscript's own bibliography and was not independently re-verified against primary sources for this build — verification is deferred to the author team's own clinician review. See the References section and METHODOLOGY.md for details.
